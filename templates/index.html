🧪 The Experimental Control Triad (Essential Grant Benchmark)
Zero-Tolerance Control Audit

Proposals lacking all three controls are routinely rejected. Explicitly specify each leg of the triad:

1. Negative / Vehicle Control
📌 What is this? A test with everything EXCEPT your active engineered tool.
✍️ How to write it: Name the blank buffer or empty host cell lysate that proves the assay gives zero signal without your tool.

📌 What is this? A test with a proven gold-standard that you know works.

✍️ How to write it: Name an inactive mutant (e.g. catalytic-dead mutant) or non-target substrate to prove reactions aren't random.
